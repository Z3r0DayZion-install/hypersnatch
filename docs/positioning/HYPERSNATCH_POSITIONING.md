# HyperSnatch Positioning

## Positioning Statement

HyperSnatch is an operator-grade forensic workflow platform for lawful evidence analysis, case organization, trust assessment, and deterministic reporting/export.

## Target Value

- Reduces analyst friction from intake to case output
- Improves confidence through explicit state, trust, and lineage signals
- Produces structured outputs suitable for review, handoff, and audit

## Why It Is Defensible

- Workflow depth across queue, case, trust, reporting, and export
- Deterministic report structure and lineage model
- UI proof gates tied to functional workflow requirements
- Release-proof discipline and reproducibility practices

## Ideal Users

- Digital forensics operators
- Incident and threat analysis teams
- Research and investigation teams needing case-centric workflow discipline

## Message Discipline

Lead with:

- lawful forensic workflow
- evidence organization and analysis
- operator trust and explainability
- deterministic proof and release discipline

Avoid framing as generalized extraction or bypass tooling.
