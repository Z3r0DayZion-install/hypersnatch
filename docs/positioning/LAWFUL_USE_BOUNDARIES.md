# Lawful Use Boundaries

## Purpose

This document defines positioning and usage boundaries for HyperSnatch as a lawful forensic workflow and evidence analysis system.

## Allowed Usage Contexts

- authorized digital forensics and incident investigation
- lawful evidence organization and reporting
- analyst workflow support for case tracking and review
- research and validation within permitted legal and contractual scope

## Prohibited Positioning

HyperSnatch must not be positioned or marketed as:

- bypassware
- piracy tooling
- unauthorized access tooling
- "get anything from anywhere" software

## Truthfulness Requirements

- status messaging must reflect actual outcomes
- warning/failed/manual-review/canceled states must be explicit
- reports must preserve uncertainty and risk context

## Operator Responsibility

Operators are responsible for:

- ensuring authorization for data handling and analysis
- respecting legal, policy, and contractual constraints
- conducting manual review where required by risk signals

## Product Boundary

HyperSnatch supports lawful forensic workflow. It does not grant access rights and does not substitute for legal/compliance review.
