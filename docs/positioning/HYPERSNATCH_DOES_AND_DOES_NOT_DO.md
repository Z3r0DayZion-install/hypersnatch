# HyperSnatch Does and Does Not Do

## HyperSnatch Does

- organize operator workflow from intake through queue, case, and report/export
- provide explicit lifecycle status and reason chains for operational transparency
- support case-linked evidence review with trust and lineage context
- generate structured deterministic reports in Markdown and JSON
- maintain verification discipline through test and UI proof gates

## HyperSnatch Does Not Do

- claim legal authority, legal conclusions, or legal admissibility outcomes
- guarantee success extraction from arbitrary targets
- present incomplete/inconclusive states as success
- position itself as unauthorized access, bypass, or piracy tooling
- replace analyst judgment for manual-review and risk decisions

## Operational Boundaries

- Warning, failed, canceled, and manual-review statuses are first-class outputs.
- Risk states remain visible in UI and report content.
- Export outputs preserve workflow truth; they do not sanitize away uncertainty.
