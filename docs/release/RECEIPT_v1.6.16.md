# HyperSnatch v1.6.16 -- Final Release Receipt

**v1.6.16 supersedes v1.6.15 as the public release.**

## Release reason

`v1.6.16 = portable proof + tamper verification`

HyperSnatch exports are now portable and self-proving. Every exported proof bundle ships an identity card (Proof Passport), can be re-verified from disk at any time (Prove It Again), and includes a standalone offline verifier (`VERIFY-HYPERSNATCH.html`) that a recipient can open in any browser with no install, account, or network. A new Tamper Trial damages a temporary copy of an export and proves HyperSnatch catches the tampering, while leaving the real export untouched. The release also adds customizable Appearance/themes and improved dynamic feedback (toasts, status, recent activity), and preserves the v1.6.15 first-run proof workflow.

## CSP

`script-src 'self'` preserved (no `unsafe-inline`). `style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'` remains intentionally deferred (separate hardening lane). The exported `VERIFY-HYPERSNATCH.html` is generated by the main process and is the exported artifact, not the app renderer, so its inline CSS/JS does not affect the app CSP promise.
